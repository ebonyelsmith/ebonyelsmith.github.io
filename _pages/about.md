I am a fifth year PhD student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences (EECS) at UC Berkeley, where I am advised by [Claire J. Tomlin](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~tomlin/) and [Gireeja Ranade](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~gireeja/).

My research lies at the intersection of control, machine learning, and formal methods, with a focus on understanding and ensuring the safety of learning-based systems. I develop methods for probabilistic verification, learning-based control, and safe autonomous systems grounded in applications to robotics and multi-agent systems.
